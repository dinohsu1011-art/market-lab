The comp: buy #1, hold while it stays #1

Same scorecard, opposite end. At each month end it buys the name with the best average rank and keeps it until another name takes the top spot. A holding tied for #1 is kept. Same checks, same month-end close, so the two lines are a fair fight.

Read the record with these in mind

  • The list was picked with hindsight. "Magnificent 7" was coined in May 2023 for seven stocks that had already won. Any rotation inside it is flattered, most of all before 2023. That stretch is context, not a track record.
  • It holds one stock. Drawdowns are single-name drawdowns, and one bad surprise hits the whole line.
  • Timing. Each trade is scored and filled at the same month-end close. In practice that means a market-on-close order on the last trading day.
  • No costs, slippage or taxes. Price return only, dividends excluded.
