Regime component backtest

Signals are calculated after the close. Returns start at the following close and run for 5, 10, 20 or 60 trading sessions. The score rules and equal weights were not changed.

The earlier period ends before January 2025. The later-period check starts in January 2025. Earlier signals whose outcomes cross that boundary are excluded.

What this can and cannot tell us

This checks whether a score describes conditions associated with better subsequent index returns. It does not establish that bullish conditions are the best entry points: weak readings may precede rebounds, and strong trends may already be priced in.

Intervals resample stretches of market history together, because nearby signals share many of the same future sessions. The CSV also includes a non-overlapping check and an unconditional benchmark average for the same dates.

Current index membership, not point-in-time; survivorship bias.

Current dataset excludes owner-removed names retrospectively; before-removals sensitivity also reported.

Later-period check is not a truly untouched prospective test; rules were designed with recent markets visible.

Index price returns exclude dividends, fees, slippage and financing; no tradable strategy CAGR claimed.

Many components/horizons are examined; confidence intervals are not corrected for multiple testing.

Overlapping signals are not independent trades. Small state samples are inconclusive.
