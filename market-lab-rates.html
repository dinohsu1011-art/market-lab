QQQ vs the 10-year yield

Weekly Friday closes. The bottom pane asks one question: over the past year, when yields rose, did QQQ rise with them or fall? Above zero, the two moved together. Below zero, higher yields were hurting tech.

Year by year

How QQQ did, how far the 10-year moved, and how tightly the two were linked on average that year.

How the link is measured. Each week takes QQQ's % change and the yield's change in points. The line is the correlation of those two over the trailing 52 weeks. It runs from −1 (always opposite) to +1 (always together).

Correlation shows direction, not size. A −0.2 reading means a loose tendency, not a rule. The 10-year is Yahoo's ^TNX, quoted in percent.
