Blueprint

  1. Nov 11 1

    Set a plan, follow the plan.

    Every trade names the plan it came from. A trade without one is off plan.

Every plan holds

  • Triggerthe price or event that starts the trade
  • Actionwhat gets bought or shorted, and nothing beyond it
  • Exit if wrongthe price or event that says the idea failed
  • Targetwhere it comes off if it works
  • Sizehow much
  • Tied tothe call the trade is built on
1

Gospel

pencil until kept
  • Buy strength on the pullback. When the market retests its lows, buy the names that held up best.
  • Short weakness into the rally. When the market is stretched up, short the names that bounced least.
  • Fade a stretched move. A stock far above its range on RSI tends to give some back.
  • Squeezes run to a target, then the next catalyst turns them.
  • The big picture sets the tone. No fresh read on cloud spending until the big cloud providers report, so expect chop and trade the range.
  • Keep the list short. Trade only the names in the plan.
