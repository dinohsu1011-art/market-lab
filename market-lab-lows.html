Near their lows

Stocks that fell 30% or more and sit near their 52-week low. Watch means still on the low. Turning means it has lifted off: the 5-day average is back over the 20-day, but it has won back under 45% of the fall. Then the harder question, theme by theme: why did it fall, and is it ripe?

  1. Do the fundamentals first.
  2. Don't buy the first dip. Wait for sellers to dry up.
  3. Buy the right side of the V: 5-day over 20-day, a base, a higher low.
  4. Waterfall declines are the riskier setup.
  5. Lean contrarian: capitulation, downgrades.

The scan

Every stock that passes, refreshed with each close. The four letters are the checks that make a turn believable: H higher low, B base of 3+ weeks, R retest of the 20-day held, V volume dried up since the low.

Groups that fell together

Industries with three or more names in the scan. A whole group at its lows usually means one shared cause, not a company problem.

How to read this

  • The scan is arithmetic and reruns with every database update. The theme verdicts are research, refreshed weekly.
  • Peak and low are taken inside the last 252 sessions. The low must be within the last ~9 months.
  • Names outside a theme basket need a $2B+ market cap and $15M+ of median daily trading.
  • A read of the data and the news, not advice to buy or sell.
