How the lines are built

  • The Citrindex starts at the Nov 4 close. Its last rebalance traded after that close, and nothing has traded since. The holdings file gives each stock's weight at the Nov 6 close; those weights are walked back to Nov 4 by each stock's move, then left to drift. Nothing is rebalanced.
  • Size and net. Size is a theme's share of the index, and the twelve add to 100%. Net is its longs minus its shorts as a share of the index. Dynamic AI is a big book that is close to market-neutral, so its net is small. A theme's "adds" is the points it put on the index.
  • Returns are in US dollars. Foreign stocks are converted at each day's exchange rate, as the index itself is. Cash earns nothing. Price return, no dividends or costs.
  • The long/short ideas start at the Oct 30 close. A pair is 100% long and 100% short, in local currency. Fiscal Primacy is 100% long against 40% short. Boomer Software is long only; the issue does not size its IGV short.
  • CCXI has no close before Nov 4 and Yahoo has no close for it since, so it uses the holdings file's Nov 6 price.
