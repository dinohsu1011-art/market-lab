Citrini ideas

The pairs and baskets from Citrini Research's Nov 4, 2026 issue, each marked buy-and-hold from the close before. A pair reads as the long's return minus the short's. Baskets keep the issue's weights. Tracked ideas, not positions.

Every line

Ranked by return since the base close. Click a line to chart its legs and see what each one added.

How the lines are built

  • Buy-and-hold from a fixed close. The long/short ideas start at the Oct 30 close, the last one before November. The State of the Themes baskets start at the Nov 3 close, the date of their weights. Nothing is rebalanced.
  • Weights are the issue's. A pair is 100% long and 100% short. Fiscal Primacy is 100% long against 40% short, so it is not dollar-neutral. A leg's contribution is its weight times its return.
  • Cash earns nothing. The Life Sciences cash sleeve (5.3%) and Paid to Wait's cash are left out of the return.
  • Boomer Software is long only. The issue runs it against an IGV short but does not give the size.
  • Price return in local currency. No dividends, no FX, no costs. CCXI has no close before Nov 4, so it is marked from its first close.
