How the calls sounded

Counts of upbeat words (such as strong, record, ahead of plan), cautious words (such as headwinds, softness, pushouts) and hedging words (such as may, too early, limited visibility) for every 10,000 words management spoke. Net is upbeat minus cautious. A word right after “no” or “not” counts the other way, so “no slowdown” is upbeat, and legal boilerplate is left out. Net in answers scores only the replies to analysts, which are less scripted than the opening remarks. Dodges counts times management declined to answer. It is still a rough gauge, so the points above come from reading the calls.
