Rules & coverage

Market trend requires three consecutive closes above the 21-day EMA; below it for three closes is a downtrend. A mixed window is labeled Mixed. Market risk always shows the wider market.

The attention list requires a signed score of +99 or higher, or −97 or lower, and five-session USD volume at least 1.5× its prior baseline. New triggers rank first, followed by volume pace and extension magnitude. Strengthening / fading compares the absolute score with the previous close. These are observations, with no estimated reversal probability.

Volume pace compares the latest five sessions with the preceding 252, requiring all five recent readings and at least 20 baseline readings. Accelerating members have pace ≥1.5× and a rising pace. Group pace divides summed recent member averages by summed baseline averages. At least half the stock members need fresh volume data. Contributor share is a share of recent volume, not of the increase.

Extreme duration uses consecutive sessions beyond the threshold. Average and median use completed same-side streaks across available indicator history; n is the sample count. Missing scores, prices and FX stay unavailable. Foreign markets may have a different latest session.

Cloud basket membership is loaded for your account. Scopes apply to stocks, including stock members of nested themes; ETFs and indices remain outside the attention list. Upcoming earnings reflect stored dates and may be incomplete.

Follow-through begins when a trigger is observed here. First-observed rows establish a baseline, without claiming the trigger began that day. Repeated updates preserve the session’s trigger list. Earlier unsigned extension alerts are excluded. Returns use stored split-adjusted closes, exclude dividends and costs, and are not executable-entry backtests.
